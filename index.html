About Bosnia and Herzegovina

A Country Full of History and Nature

Bosnia and Herzegovina is located in the heart of the Balkans. It is known for its rich history, beautiful mountains, crystal-clear rivers and diverse cultural heritage.

From the historic streets of Sarajevo to the famous Old Bridge in Mostar, the waterfalls of Una and the mountains surrounding the country, Bosnia and Herzegovina offers something for everyone.
